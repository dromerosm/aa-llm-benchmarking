Methodology description

Artificial Analysis independently benchmarks model intelligence, quality, performance, and price across models and endpoints, and defines its performance metrics as end-to-end customer experience across providers rather than theoretical peak hardware throughput; attribution to Artificial Analysis is required when reusing data from the free API.

Reading Guide

How To Read This View

Points further right are newer releases. Points higher up have higher Artificial Analysis intelligence scores. Larger circles indicate more expensive blended pricing.

Bubble size uses a square-root scale so ultra-expensive frontier models do not visually dominate the whole chart.
